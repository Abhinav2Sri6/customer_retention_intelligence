# Customer Retention Intelligence

An end-to-end data science project focused on identifying customer churn risk, understanding the drivers of attrition, and developing data-driven retention strategies for a telecommunications company.

## Project Objectives

- Identify customers at risk of churn
- Analyze behavioral, account, and service factors associated with churn
- Quantify customer and revenue risk
- Build machine learning models to predict churn
- Develop a customer retention priority system
- Incorporate AI-generated explanations and retention recommendations
